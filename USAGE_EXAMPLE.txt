🎓 UNDERSTANDING THE MODEL:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The autoencoder learned what NORMAL transactions look like.
When it sees something unusual:
- High reconstruction error = Can't rebuild it = ANOMALY = FRAUD

Think of it like:
- Model: "I've seen 40K normal transactions"
- New transaction: "This pattern is weird, I can't reconstruct it!"
- Result: HIGH ERROR → FRAUD
